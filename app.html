    Calculation detailsSpeed relative to #1 at defeating all 3 Rocket Pokémon with fast moves only, averaged over every possible lineup. Assumes level 50 hundos. * Elite or legacy move. Grunt stats: Pokebattler 2021 formula, measured up to trainer level 50 (higher levels use the level 50 values). Leader stats are ×1.05, Giovanni's ×1.15.

    Calculation detailsScore relative to #1, based on the time to defeat the boss alone with six copies of the same level 50 hundo, averaged over the boss's possible movesets. Includes damage taken, energy from damage, fainting, switching (1 s) and rejoining after six faints (10 s), with the boss attacking every 2 s plus move duration. Megas include their own mega boost and max mega level (mega moves at 130% power, as on Pokebattler). All time gives 5 points for 1st, 4 for 2nd, 3 for 3rd–4th, 2 for 5th–8th and 1 for 9th–16th place against every released legendary, mythical and mega as a raid boss. Current does the same for the current 5★ and mega bosses. * Elite or legacy move. Boss HP and stat multipliers: Pokebattler.
