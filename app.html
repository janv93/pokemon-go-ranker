Calculation details

      Ranks this Pokémon among all Pokémon of the same type, once for each of its types. Level 50 hundos. Against each opponent the best scores 100%, and the rank comes from the average over all opponents. The % is relative to #1.

      Rocket: fast move of that type, calculated like the Rocket tab at the trainer level above, against every grunt, leader and Giovanni whose lineup that type hits super effectively on average. Megas are left out.

      Raid: the best moveset with a fast or charged move of that type, simulated like the Raid tab, against every all-time boss (5★ legendaries, mythicals, ultra beasts and megas) and every current 4★ or stronger raid boss that is weak to that type.

      Max: Max Attack of that type (from the fast move, or the Pokémon's own G-Max or special Max Move), calculated like the Max tab, against every 5★, 6★ and current Max boss weak to that type. Only Pokémon that can Dynamax take part. Tanks and healers: against every 5★, 6★ and current Max boss, calculated like the Max tab.

      Moves: the ones used against most opponents. * Elite or legacy move.

      The strip shows everyone it's ranked against by average score (one tick each, right is best); the highlighted tick with the arrow is this Pokémon. Hover over or tap the strip to see who each tick is.

      Calculation details

      Speed relative to #1 at defeating all 3 Rocket Pokémon with fast moves only, averaged over every possible lineup. Assumes level 50 hundos.

      Grunt stats: Pokebattler 2021 formula, measured up to trainer level 50 (higher levels use the level 50 values). Leader stats are ×1.05, Giovanni's ×1.15.

      Calculation details

      Score relative to #1 of its group, based on your damage per second with six copies of the same level 50 hundo, averaged over the boss's possible movesets.

      Megas and primals are ranked separately and only the top 5 are shown, since you can bring only one per raid; the rest of the list fills your other five slots.

      4★, mega and stronger bosses assume an average remote lobby of 5, 7 or 10 players, where the others bring the 10th best non-mega counter at level 40 and everyone has a Good Friend bonus (3%), so the fastest raid is the one where you deal the most damage before the boss falls. Weaker bosses assume you raid alone.

      Includes every boss and attacker hit, damage taken, energy from damage, fainting, switching (1 s) and rejoining after six faints (15 s), with the boss attacking every 2 s plus move duration. Megas include their own mega boost; it doesn't apply to your other attackers.

      All time gives 5 points for 1st, 4 for 2nd, 3 for 3rd–4th, 2 for 5th–8th and 1 for 9th–16th place within each group against every released legendary, mythical and mega as a raid boss. Current does the same for the current 5★ and mega bosses.

      * Elite or legacy move. Boss HP and stat multipliers: Pokebattler.

      Calculation details

      Score relative to #1 of its group, for level 50 hundos with Max Moves at level 3.

      Attackers: damage of one Max Attack (power 350; G-Max moves and Dynamax Cannon 450). A Dynamax Pokémon's Max Attack has the type of its fast move, so the best fast move for this boss is shown.

      Tanks: Max Meter charge they build before fainting. Each hit adds 1 charge per 0.5% of the boss's HP it deals (times the boss's meter multiplier), at least 1, so fast hits count most; the best fast move or fast and charged combination is used.

      Healers: Max Spirit heals every ally by 16% of the healer's max HP, scaled down if the healer survives fewer boss attacks than most. Survival is against the boss's average regular charged move.

      Dodging, Max Guard and damage bonuses help everyone equally and are left out. Pre-evolutions are hidden when their evolution can also Dynamax.

      All time gives 5 points for 1st, 4 for 2nd, 3 for 3rd–4th, 2 for 5th–8th and 1 for 9th–16th place within each group against every 5★ and 6★ Max boss so far.

      * Elite or legacy move. Boss HP, CPM and meter multiplier: Pokebattler.
