https://janv93.github.io/pokemon-go-ranker/

Installable as an app (PWA).

Pokémon GO rankings, always up to date with data from PvPoke, ScrapedDuck, Pokebattler and the game master:

- **IV**: how your IVs rank in Little, Great, Ultra and Master League.
- **Auto**: pick a Pokémon and see how it ranks among Pokémon of its own type in Rocket, Raid and Max Battles.
- **Rocket**: the fastest Pokémon to farm Team GO Rocket with fast moves only.
- **Raid**: the best attackers against each current raid boss, plus all-time and current rankings for 5★ and mega raids.
- **Max**: the best attackers, tanks and healers against each current Max Battle boss, plus an all-time ranking for 5★ and 6★ bosses.
